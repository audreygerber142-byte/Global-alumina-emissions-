Scientific model

Live: mission, NET, status, provider, rocket, pad and coordinates are fetched from Launch Library 2. Curated: propulsion chemistry, propellant mass and aluminum fraction are stored locally and never inferred from the launch API.

Emission indices: Brown et al. (2024) reports HTPB-solid indices of 380 g Al₂O₃/kg propellant and 20 g BC/kg; its likely submicron-alumina value is 60 g/kg. The same paper reports 20 g BC/kg for RP-1/LOX. These are model estimates, not direct measurements of an individual future plume.

V3.1 aluminum rule: manufacturer/published motor fractions are used where available; otherwise V3 supplies a clearly labeled central engineering estimate rather than a missing value. Open a card's scientific details to see the basis.
